Messages are classified into fast (gpt-4.1), standard (claude-sonnet-4.6), or premium (claude-opus-4.6) tiers by a dedicated GPT-4.1 classifier session with 8s timeout. Design keywords (ui, ux, css, layout, etc.) always override to premium via word-boundary regex matching. A 2-message cooldown prevents tier flapping from borderline messages. Short follow-ups (<20 chars matching exact patterns like 'yes', 'do it', 'sure') inherit the previous tier. Background task results always classify as standard. Manual model switch disables auto-routing until re-enabled.
